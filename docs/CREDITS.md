# Credits

- **Design:** Jean Hiraga, *Le Petit*, published in *L'Audiophile* no. 29 (1983).
- **Hole positions:** after R. Berchtold (2022).
- **This adaptation:** a 22 mm OSB version of the plan (3 sheets, A3), a cut layout for one
  2000 × 1000 mm sheet, and a measured tuning for the Aiyima 4″ full-range driver.
- **Methods:** synchronized swept-sine measurement after A. Novak et al. (JAES 2015); near-field
  assessment after D. B. Keele (JAES 1974).

Thank you to everyone who has built Le Petit and shared plans, drawings and listening notes — this
project stands on that work.
